## Description (paste into the mission submission)
The Old Zoo Mystery is a multiplayer mystery party game set in the
fictional old city of Velmara. Overnight, a star animal vanishes from
the Old Zoo's locked enclosure, and one of the zoo staff did it.

2 to 8 players join from their phones with a 4-letter room code. No
signup. No app. Everyone gets a secret staff card, and one of you is
the culprit. Short on humans? Scripted staff suspects fill the empty
chairs, and you can interrogate them in character.

Three clue rounds with table talk. Open interrogation, where the Head
Keeper drops spoiler-free hints. Then you accuse and vote. Majority
wins. Ties let the culprit walk. The accused gets a final plea before
the reveal, where the true story comes out and everyone's secrets are
exposed.

Built with Node.js and Socket.IO, deployed on Render. It runs at zero
cost: six hand-written cases power the game with no API key at all, so
every game is fully playable the moment the link opens.
